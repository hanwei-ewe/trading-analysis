How to choose parameters

Rule of thumb — faster vs slower. Keep each indicator faster than the trend it confirms, and each "fast" line shorter than its own "slow" line.

  • EMA — trend anchor; slow is fine here. 20/50/200 (faster style: 9/21/50). These set direction.
  • MACD = two EMAs (fast/slow) plus a signal. Keep them below your trend EMAs so momentum leads — e.g. 12/26/9 against EMA 20/50/200. If MACD slow ≥ your EMA period, the two signals duplicate.
  • RSI — momentum, 0–100. Typical 7–21 (14 default). Longer = smoother/slower, shorter = twitchier. Keep it shorter than your trend EMAs.
  • Stochastic — range position. %K period then %D smoothing; keep %D < %K (e.g. 14/3/3). Leads price most, noisiest.
  • Bollinger — volatility envelope. Period ~20, width 2σ. Wider σ = fewer, larger band breaks.
  • ADX — trend strength (not direction), ~14. Longer period updates more slowly.
  • VWAP — where volume traded. Window should match your horizon; shorter = more reactive.
  • OBV — cumulative volume; the lookback only sets how far back "rising/falling" is judged.

There is no single correct setting. What matters is that the periods are internally consistent and match your horizon. Warnings here are advice, not rules — you can ignore them once you understand why they fired.
